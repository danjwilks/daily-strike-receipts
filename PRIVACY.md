# Privacy policy: Daily Strike receipts

Daily Strike receipts is a personal tool used only by its owner.

- **What it reads:** with read-only Gmail access, only the owner's bank alert emails, student-loan payment confirmations and receipt photos the owner sends to themselves. No other email is read, and nothing in the mailbox is changed or deleted.
- **What it does with it:** updates the owner's personal budget, which is stored on the owner's own computer.
- **Sharing:** receipt photos are sent to an AI service to read the items. Nothing else is shared, sold or used for any other purpose.
- **Revoking access:** at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: November 8, 2026.
